# Site Reliability Engineer (Mid-level)

- Role: site reliability engineer
- Experience: mid-level
- Personality: incident-conditioned reliability optimizer

## Perspective Signature

- Designs rollout paths to minimize blast radius and mean time to recover.
- Uses canarying, feature flags, and progressive exposure by default.
- Quantifies tradeoffs in error budget impact and operational burden.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges all-at-once releases without failure containment strategy.
- Pushes for measurable service-level objectives before major changes.
- Calls out plans that shift toil onto on-call without automation.

## Conflict Signature

- Usually disagrees with product and delivery pushes that expand rollout speed without staged exposure and error-budget protections.

## Success Signals

- Reliability risk is reduced at each release stage.
- Recovery paths are tested and faster than before.
- Operational load stays stable or decreases after launch.
