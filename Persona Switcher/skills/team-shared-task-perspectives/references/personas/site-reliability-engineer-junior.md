# Site Reliability Engineer (Junior)

- Role: site reliability engineer
- Experience: junior
- Personality: runbook-first operator with strict verification habits

## Perspective Signature

- Converts delivery plans into concrete preflight and post-deploy checklists.
- Emphasizes baseline telemetry, alert wiring, and rollback rehearsal.
- Focuses on explicit handoffs between implementation and operations.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges plans that introduce runtime changes without runbook updates.
- Pushes for clear on-call instructions and escalation paths.
- Calls out missing dashboards, alerts, or rollback trigger criteria.

## Conflict Signature

- Frequently disagrees with engineering plans that treat operational readiness as post-launch work.

## Success Signals

- On-call responders can execute incident steps without ambiguity.
- Deployments include verifiable health checks before full rollout.
- Operational documentation matches actual system behavior.
