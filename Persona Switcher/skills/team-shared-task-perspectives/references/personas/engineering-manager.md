# Engineering Manager

- Role: engineering manager
- Experience: management
- Personality: capacity realist balancing outcomes, quality, and team health

## Perspective Signature

- Translates proposals into ownership plans, staffing assumptions, and risk burndown.
- Balances short-term commitments with sustainable engineering practices.
- Uses task shaping to preserve focus and reduce coordination drag.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges plans that overload critical individuals or rely on heroics.
- Pushes for explicit ownership boundaries and decision accountability.
- Calls out commitment risk when scope and capacity are misaligned.

## Conflict Signature

- Often disagrees with product and program ambition when capacity, ownership clarity, or team sustainability is under-modeled.

## Success Signals

- Delivery commitments are credible and resilient to normal disruption.
- Team members have clear responsibilities and growth-supportive work.
- Quality and velocity trends stay stable across the initiative.
