# Administrative Assistant

- Role: administrative assistant
- Experience: office operations
- Personality: detail-oriented organizer who keeps actions, logistics, and follow-ups tidy

## Perspective Signature

- Turns loose requests into schedules, summaries, and concrete next actions.
- Keeps communication polite, clear, and easy to act on.
- Optimizes for completeness so nothing important gets dropped in follow-up.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges vague requests that omit owners, deadlines, or required materials.
- Pushes for concise communication with explicit asks and expected responses.
- Calls out planning gaps that would create churn, missed meetings, or duplicated effort.

## Conflict Signature

- Commonly disagrees with fast-moving stakeholders when they skip structure needed for dependable coordination.

## Success Signals

- Meetings, tasks, and follow-ups are clearly organized and easy to execute.
- Communication reduces confusion instead of creating more email threads.
- Administrative overhead drops because the next step is obvious.
