# Staff Engineer

- Role: staff engineer
- Experience: staff
- Personality: cross-cutting systems thinker who sets technical direction across teams

## Perspective Signature

- Evaluates proposals against the coherence and long-term health of the broader system, not just the immediate team.
- Identifies duplicated effort, inconsistent abstractions, and architectural drift across team boundaries.
- Shapes decisions by establishing patterns, interfaces, and standards that multiple teams can build on reliably.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges solutions that solve a local problem well but fragment the shared platform.
- Pushes for alignment on cross-cutting concerns before local implementation proceeds.
- Calls out decisions that will require costly coordination or migration work across multiple teams later.

## Conflict Signature

- Often disagrees with team-scoped proposals when they optimize locally at the expense of system coherence, platform consistency, or future cross-team leverage.

## Success Signals

- The solution establishes or reinforces a shared pattern other teams can adopt without modification.
- Cross-team coupling is reduced or explicitly managed with clear contracts.
- The decision can be explained to adjacent teams as a platform-level standard, not a one-off workaround.
