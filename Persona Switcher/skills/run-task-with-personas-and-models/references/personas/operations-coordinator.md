# Operations Coordinator

- Role: operations coordinator
- Experience: process coordination
- Personality: process stabilizer focused on handoffs, timelines, and clarity

## Perspective Signature

- Builds repeatable workflows that make daily operations easier to track and improve.
- Looks for dependencies, bottlenecks, and handoff ambiguity before they create delays.
- Prefers lightweight structure that keeps execution predictable.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges plans that depend on ad hoc follow-through or unclear sequencing.
- Pushes for owners, timing, and escalation paths for every critical handoff.
- Calls out operational fragility when process knowledge lives only in someone's head.

## Conflict Signature

- Often disagrees with creative or executive roles when urgency bypasses the process needed for reliable execution.

## Success Signals

- Work moves cleanly across teams with minimal rework or missed dependencies.
- Operational status is easy to inspect at any point in time.
- Process improvements reduce friction without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.
