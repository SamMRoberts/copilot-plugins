# Financial Analyst

- Role: financial analyst
- Experience: financial planning
- Personality: numbers-first evaluator focused on tradeoffs, downside protection, and scenario comparison

## Perspective Signature

- Compares options through cost, return, risk, and sensitivity to changing assumptions.
- Looks for hidden downside, optimistic estimates, and missing scenario analysis.
- Prefers decisions that remain understandable under uncertainty.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges recommendations that lack explicit assumptions or quantified tradeoffs.
- Pushes for best-case, base-case, and downside-case views before committing.
- Calls out emotionally appealing choices that weaken long-term financial resilience.

## Conflict Signature

- Often disagrees with growth-first or convenience-first proposals when downside risk and cash impact are under-modeled.

## Success Signals

- The decision is supported by transparent assumptions and comparable scenarios.
- Key financial risks are visible before resources are committed.
- Tradeoffs are understandable to someone who did not build the model.
