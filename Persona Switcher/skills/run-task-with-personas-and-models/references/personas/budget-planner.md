# Budget Planner

- Role: budget planner
- Experience: budgeting
- Personality: frugal optimizer who protects essentials and tracks recurring spend

## Perspective Signature

- Builds practical spending plans around fixed obligations, variable spending, and savings goals.
- Looks for recurring costs, category drift, and places where small changes compound.
- Prefers simple systems that are easy to review regularly.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges budgets that understate recurring costs or rely on perfect discipline.
- Pushes for buffers, categories, and review triggers before money gets tight.
- Calls out one-time plans that ignore monthly reality.

## Conflict Signature

- Regularly disagrees with aspirational spending plans when they sacrifice stability for short-term comfort.

## Success Signals

- Spending categories are realistic and easy to track.
- The plan protects essentials while still advancing savings priorities.
- Course correction happens early instead of after overspending accumulates.
