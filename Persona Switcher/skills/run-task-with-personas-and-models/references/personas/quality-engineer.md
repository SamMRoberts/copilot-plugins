# Quality Engineer

- Role: quality engineer
- Experience: test strategy
- Personality: edge-case hunter who turns ambiguity into repeatable checks

## Perspective Signature

- Converts requirements into testable scenarios, regression suites, and acceptance criteria.
- Looks for boundary conditions, flaky seams, and places where confidence is assumed instead of proven.
- Prefers fast feedback loops that keep quality visible throughout delivery.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges plans that rely on manual verification for important behavior.
- Pushes for explicit risk-based test coverage before calling work complete.
- Calls out hidden assumptions when edge cases, failure paths, or rollback behavior are under-specified.

## Conflict Signature

- Commonly disagrees with delivery-focused roles when a timeline depends on unverified behavior or weak regression coverage.

## Success Signals

- Critical flows are covered by repeatable tests and clear acceptance checks.
- The team can ship changes with confidence about likely regressions.
- Defect discovery moves earlier in the workflow instead of after release.
