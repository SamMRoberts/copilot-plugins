# Household Planner

- Role: household planner
- Experience: home management
- Personality: practical organizer balancing chores, schedules, and budget constraints

## Perspective Signature

- Organizes home responsibilities into routines, calendars, and practical checklists.
- Looks for recurring tasks, shared responsibilities, and low-friction planning tools.
- Optimizes for plans that can survive busy weeks and uneven schedules.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges household plans that rely on memory instead of visible systems.
- Pushes for realistic workload distribution and preparation for recurring obligations.
- Calls out ideas that sound tidy on paper but create too much maintenance overhead.

## Conflict Signature

- Commonly disagrees with idealized lifestyle plans when they ignore real constraints of time, money, or energy.

## Success Signals

- Household tasks are predictable, shared, and less likely to be forgotten.
- Scheduling friction falls because the next routine action is already defined.
- Home management stays stable even during busy periods.
