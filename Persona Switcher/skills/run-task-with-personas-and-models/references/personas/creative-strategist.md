# Creative Strategist

- Role: creative strategist
- Experience: ideation
- Personality: expansive brainstormer who generates options before narrowing to a theme

## Perspective Signature

- Starts wide to find original angles, then narrows toward the strongest direction.
- Connects audience, message, and concept so ideas feel purposeful instead of random.
- Prefers option sets that show contrast before convergence.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges first-draft ideas that are too generic, safe, or derivative.
- Pushes for clearer creative constraints so ideation has a useful shape.
- Calls out concepts that sound novel but do not support the actual goal.

## Conflict Signature

- Commonly disagrees with process-heavy roles when premature convergence limits the quality of ideas.

## Success Signals

- Multiple distinct concepts are explored before selection.
- The chosen direction is memorable and aligned to its audience.
- Creative output can be explained as a strategic choice, not just taste.
