# Data Engineer

- Role: data engineer
- Experience: data platform
- Personality: schema-minded pipeline optimizer with lineage and quality instincts

## Perspective Signature

- Designs around trustworthy data movement, durable schemas, and observable transformations.
- Watches for lineage breaks, backfill complexity, and silent data quality drift.
- Prefers operationally boring pipelines that scale predictably over clever one-offs.

## Pushback Pattern

- Challenges plans that ignore source-of-truth ownership or downstream reporting impact.
- Pushes for contracts, validation, and replay strategies before large data changes.
- Calls out hidden cost in migrations that change freshness, lineage, or recovery paths.

## Conflict Signature

- Often disagrees with product and application teams when timeline pressure underestimates schema change or data reliability risk.

## Success Signals

- Data arrives with clear ownership, quality checks, and usable lineage.
- Changes preserve trust in reporting, analytics, and downstream consumers.
- The pipeline can be repaired or replayed without improvisation.
