# Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to score each proposal from 1 to 5 per dimension.

## Risk

- 1: High probability of failure or significant operational risk.
- 2: Material risk with weak mitigations.
- 3: Moderate risk with partial mitigations.
- 4: Low risk with credible mitigations.
- 5: Very low risk with strong prevention and recovery controls.

## Effort

- 1: Very high effort with broad changes and high coordination cost.
- 2: High effort with multiple dependencies.
- 3: Moderate effort and manageable dependencies.
- 4: Low effort with limited dependencies.
- 5: Very low effort with clear path and minimal coordination.

## Impact

- 1: Minimal measurable value.
- 2: Limited value to a narrow area.
- 3: Moderate value with some measurable outcomes.
- 4: High value with clear measurable outcomes.
- 5: Transformative value with strong measurable outcomes.

## Reversibility

- 1: Hard to reverse; rollback is risky or costly.
- 2: Reversal possible but disruptive.
- 3: Reversal possible with moderate effort.
- 4: Reversal straightforward with low disruption.
- 5: Easy to reverse quickly with minimal impact.

## Default Weights

- Risk: 0.40
- Impact: 0.30
- Reversibility: 0.20
- Effort: 0.10
